4. HOW TO ENTER

Entry is free. Entrants choose one of two themes and submit a video
directly through the entry form at kayalevents.com.au/reel-contest — no
public posting to Instagram or any other platform is required to enter:

- Team Mohanlal — dialogues, iconic scenes, or dance to his songs.
- Team Chithra — melodies, lip-syncs, or creative videos to K.S. Chithra
  songs.

Videos must be your own upload — either a file submitted directly through
the entry form (maximum 10MB) or, for larger files, a Google Drive link you
control, shared with "Anyone with the link can view" access so Kayal can
retrieve it. Videos must be no longer than 90 seconds. There is no limit on
the number of entries a person may submit, and you may enter both themes.

Entrants are solely responsible for the audio/music used in their own
account or upload — see clause 8 (Music & copyright).

5. JUDGING CRITERIA AND PROCESS

All eligible entries will be scored by a panel of three judges appointed by
Kayal Events, against the following published, weighted criteria (each
scored out of 10):

- Creativity — 40%
- Execution / effort (performance and video/audio quality) — 30%
- Entertainment value / rewatchability — 20%
- Theme fit — 10%

Each judge's scores are weighted and averaged across the panel; the five
entries with the highest average score are the winners. Judges score
entries independently from the submitted file or Drive link only — public
engagement (likes, views, shares) plays no part in judging, as entries are
not required to be posted publicly. The judging panel's decision is final
and binding, and no correspondence will be entered into regarding
individual scores.
