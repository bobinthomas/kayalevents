#KAYALREELFEST — REEL CONTEST TERMS & CONDITIONS
Kayal Events
Last updated: 8 July 2026

The Kayal Events Reel Contest is a skill-based competition conducted by
Kayal Events. Winners will be selected solely on the basis of merit and
judging criteria.

1. ELIGIBILITY

Participation is open to Australian residents aged 18 years and over.

By entering the Contest, participants confirm that their submission is
original, that they own or control the rights necessary to submit the
content, and that their entry complies with these Terms and Conditions.

2. CONTEST PERIOD

The Contest opens on 10 July 2026 and closes at 11:59 PM AEST on
18 July 2026.

Entries received after the closing date, or entries that are incomplete,
corrupted, inaccessible, or otherwise non-compliant, may be deemed invalid
at the sole discretion of Kayal Events.

3. HOW TO ENTER

Entry is free of charge.

Participants must submit their reel through the official entry form
available on the Kayal Events website. Entrants may choose to create
content inspired by either:

- Team Mohanlal
- Team Chithra

Videos must:

- Be no longer than 90 seconds in duration;
- Be submitted as a direct file upload or via a Google Drive link with
  public viewing access;
- Be the original work of the entrant or submitted with appropriate
  authority.

Multiple entries are not permitted.

4. JUDGING

All eligible entries will be reviewed by a judging panel appointed by
Kayal Events.

Entries will be assessed based on:

- Creativity
- Performance and presentation quality
- Entertainment value
- Relevance to the selected theme

The decisions of the judging panel are final and no correspondence
regarding judging outcomes will be entered into.

5. PRIZES

The top five selected entries will be professionally edited and featured
on Kayal Events' official marketing and social media channels.

The top five winning entries, whether submitted by an individual or a
group, will each receive one admission ticket to Mohanlal Live in
Australia, to be held in Sydney on 8 August 2026. Ticket category and
seating allocation will be determined by Kayal Events and are subject to
availability.

Prizes are:

- Non-transferable;
- Cannot be exchanged for cash or alternative products;
- Must not be sold, transferred, or resold.

Entrants residing outside New South Wales may additionally receive a
travel contribution voucher valued at AUD $100. This voucher is intended
solely as a contribution towards travel expenses and does not represent
reimbursement of actual travel costs. Additional terms relating to voucher
redemption may apply.
