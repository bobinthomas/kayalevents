# #KayalReelFest — Judging Rubric

For the three appointed judges. These weights are published in the contest
[Terms & Conditions](../src/app/(site)/reel-contest-terms/page.tsx) (clause 5)
— do not change them mid-contest without reissuing the terms.

Score every entry from the submitted video file or Drive link only. Public
engagement (likes, views, shares) is not a factor — entries are not required
to be posted publicly, so it wouldn't be a fair comparison.

## Criteria (score each 0–10 per entry)

| Criterion | Weight | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Creativity** | 40% | Original take on the theme — a fresh angle on a scene/song, not a straight re-enactment. Reward surprising choices, staging, or editing. |
| **Execution / effort** | 30% | Performance quality (voice, dance, comic timing) and technical quality (framing, lighting, audio sync, stability). |
| **Entertainment value / rewatchability** | 20% | Would you watch this again, or send it to a friend? Pacing and a strong hook in the first few seconds count here. |
| **Theme fit** | 10% | Clearly Team Mohanlal (dialogue/scene/dance to his songs) or Team Chithra (melody/lip-sync/creative video to her songs) — not a generic reel loosely tied to the theme. |

Weighted score per judge = `creativity×0.4 + execution×0.3 + entertainment×0.2 + theme_fit×0.1`
(computed automatically once you enter the four raw 0–10 scores).

## Where to score

Score directly in the **Judging** tab of the contest Google Sheet — one row
per entry, columns grouped per judge (`judge1_*`, `judge2_*`, `judge3_*`).
Enter only the four raw scores in your columns; `_weighted`, `total_avg`, and
`rank` are formulas and fill in automatically as all three judges score an
entry.

## Result

The five entries with the highest `total_avg` are the winners. The panel's
decision is final — see clause 5 of the Terms & Conditions.
