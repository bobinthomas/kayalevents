# #KayalReelFest — Promotion Calendar

Entry window: **Fri 10 Jul – Fri 31 Jul 2026** (3 weeks). Winners announced
**Mon 3 Aug**. Show: **Sat 8 Aug 2026**, Norwest Convention Centre, Sydney.

Because entries are private uploads (no public posting required to enter),
almost all visible contest momentum has to come from Kayal's own channels —
this calendar is written as a deliberate content pipeline, not a reactive
reposting schedule. Posting cadence below is realistic (2–4 touchpoints/week
plus daily Stories in key moments), not a forced daily feed post — a UGC
contest with private entries doesn't generate enough public activity to fill
one every day, and low-signal filler would train followers to tune out.

Data-driven posts (scoreboard, entry counts) pull from the Admin entries
dashboard (`apps-script/reel-contest/Admin.html`) — its counts bar is
exactly the Team Mohanlal vs Team Chithra tally and voucher-eligible count
needed for those posts. This is a manual "check dashboard, post the numbers"
step, not a live feed embedded in the post itself.

## Week 1 — Seed reels (Fri 17 Jul – Thu 23 Jul)

Kayal-produced or commissioned example reels — these exist to show the bar
and unstick anyone unsure what counts as a good entry. Brief for each:

1. **Team Mohanlal example** — a short dialogue re-enactment or dance clip,
   filmed simply (phone, one location), captioned "This is the bar — now
   beat it." Signals production value is not what's being judged.
2. **Team Chithra example** — a lip-sync or acoustic cover clip, same
   informal production style.
3. **Optional third seed** — a comedic/creative entry (not a straight
   re-enactment) to signal the Creativity criterion (40% of the score)
   matters most.
